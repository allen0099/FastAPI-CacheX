**Type checkers see the type of `get_or_set(type_=...)` and `get_many(type_=...)`,
and the parameters of a `@cached` method.** `get_or_set(..., type_=User)` now
returns `User` and `get_many(..., type_=User)` `dict[str, User]` to mypy and
pyright, as `get()` already did; both were `Any`. On an instance,
`obj.load(...)`, `obj.load.cache_key(...)` and `obj.load.invalidate(...)` take
the method's parameters after `self`, so wrong arguments are flagged as they
are for a plain function. Nothing changes at runtime.
