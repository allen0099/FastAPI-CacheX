**`CacheLock` refuses a `poll_interval` or `timeout` it cannot wait with.**
`poll_interval=0` made a waiting `acquire()` call the backend in a tight loop
(tens of thousands of calls a second against a held lock), and negative
values were accepted. `poll_interval` must now be positive and finite and
`timeout` `None` or non-negative, in the constructor and in `acquire()`;
anything else raises `ValueError` or `TypeError`. The docs also say that the
lock is not re-entrant.
